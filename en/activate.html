Register your ShouArchive license

This opens ShouArchive on this Mac from the link in your email.

When ShouArchive opens, it asks whether to register the license on this Mac. Click Register and you’re done.

Open in ShouArchive

The app didn’t open? Make sure you opened this page on the Mac where ShouArchive is installed. If it still doesn’t open, go to Settings › License › Enter key… in ShouArchive and enter your purchase email and the key above. Don’t have the app yet? Get it from the Download page.

No license key was found in the link. Click the link in your email again, or enter the key yourself in Settings › License › Enter key… in ShouArchive. Lost your key? Get it again.

Good to know

  • One license works on 2 Macs. If you register more, the app shows the registered Macs so you can deactivate one you no longer use.
  • This page doesn’t send your key to any server. The key is only in the part of the address after #, and it’s removed from the address once read.
