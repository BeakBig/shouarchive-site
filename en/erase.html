What erasing does

  • Every license bought with that email is revoked and every registered Mac is deactivated. At its next license check, the app locks with the notice "This license was revoked at your request to erase your personal data."
  • The purchase email, stored license keys, email delivery records, and key-change and erasure confirmation links are deleted.
  • Automatic update renewal (the subscription) is cancelled. You will not be charged again.
  • Erased licenses cannot be restored, and keys cannot be resent. To use the app again, you need to buy a new license.

What remains

  • Payment records (transaction id, type, time) are kept for 5 years as required by Korea's E-Commerce Act and then deleted. They are no longer linked to your email, keys or Macs.
  • Payment information held by Paddle (name, address, payment method, receipts) is kept separately by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Request it directly at privacy@paddle.com or paddle.net.

Good to know

  • If you want a refund, request it before erasing, under the Refund Policy.
  • If you no longer have access to the email you paid with, send the payment date and what appears on your card statement to support@shouarchive.beakbig.com. We handle it after verifying your identity.
  • What we store and when we delete it is described in Sections 5, 6 and 11 of the Privacy Policy.
