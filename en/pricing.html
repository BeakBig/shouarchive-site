Payment and updates

Sales have not started yet. Purchases open on January 1, 2027. This section describes how payment will work then — if anything changes before then, this page is updated first.

  • Payments by Paddle. Payment is handled by Paddle as the Merchant of Record. Paddle takes care of payment and tax calculation and issues receipts. Prices are listed in USD, and the checkout screen shows your local currency.
  • Tax is extra. The prices above do not include tax. At checkout, Paddle automatically calculates and adds tax for your region.
  • Buy once, keep using it. US$29 includes the license and 1 year of updates. When the update period ends, the app does not stop, and you can keep using every version released during that period.
  • Update renewal — US$15 a year. The update period renews automatically every year. You can turn off auto-renewal at any time (from the management link in Paddle's billing emails or in the app under Settings › License › "Manage billing…"). We notify you 7 days before each renewal by email and in the app. If you renew again after the period has ended, a new year starts from that payment date.
  • Versions released after the period ends. Versions released after your update period has ended can be installed and activated only after you renew. The in-app update check shows only versions released within your period.
  • 14-day trial. It starts inside the app with no payment method, and you are not charged automatically when it ends. If the trial ends without a key, profile management (including download, creation and device registration), file inspection, API key management and history (viewing records only) keep working; app build, job queue, app upload, device install, re-signing, library, IPA cleanup, OTA distribution and store release open up again when you buy.
  • Refunds. For both the first purchase and update renewals, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of payment. The details are in the Refund Policy.
  • Terms of use. Before you buy, please read the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
