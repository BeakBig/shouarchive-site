Payments and subscriptions

The commercial edition has not started yet. The price and the release date are not fixed at this point. This section describes how payment will work when the commercial edition goes live — if it changes before then, this page will be updated first.

  • Payment processor — Paddle. Payments for the commercial edition are handled by Paddle as the Merchant of Record. Paddle handles the payment and tax calculation and issues the receipt.
  • Taxes are included in the price shown. VAT or GST may apply depending on your jurisdiction, and the actual amount is calculated for your location on the checkout screen.
  • Monthly subscriptions renew automatically. When a subscription period ends, the next period starts automatically at the same price and is charged then.
  • 14-day trial. The trial starts after a payment method is added, and when it ends the monthly subscription starts automatically unless you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time. After cancelling you keep the period you already paid for, and nothing is charged from the next renewal on.
  • Refunds. When and how you can get money back is written up in the Refund Policy.
  • Conditions of use. Please read the Terms of Service before you use the commercial edition.

None of this applies to the Mac App Store edition. Apple handles payment and subscriptions, and personal data is handled under Apple's and ShouArchive's privacy policies.
