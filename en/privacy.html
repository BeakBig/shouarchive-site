Privacy Policy

ShouArchive (macOS) · Effective October 17, 2026 · Developer and seller: Beakbig

Summary. The app has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts. This site uses only cookieless visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics — aggregate figures that do not identify individuals) (Section 6). The files you inspect are read only on your Mac. App Store Connect and Google Play features connect directly to Apple and Google with keys you register yourself. The only things that reach the seller are the license key, device identifier, Mac model and app version sent when your license is checked, the purchase email when you register, ask for your key again, change it or erase your records (Section 5), the purchase details passed on by Paddle, which handles payment as Merchant of Record (Section 7), and whatever you send us yourself by support email (Section 14).

1. What we don't collect

The app does none of the following.

  • It does not collect analytics data such as usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not track you or your device, and it shows no ads.
  • There is no account or sign-in. Your license is checked with a single key.
  • It does not send the contents of the files you inspect, your project source or your build output to the seller.

2. Handling of files

The files handled by artifact inspection and profile management (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) are read only on your Mac. Their contents and anything derived from them (bundle ID, signing certificates, permission lists and so on) are only shown in the app window and are never sent anywhere. Artifact inspection does not modify the original file. Files created by building or exporting are saved only to the folder you choose.

4. What is stored on your Mac

  • App settings (theme, language, text size, project list and so on) are stored in macOS user defaults and the Application Support folder.
  • The contents of API keys are stored in this Mac's keychain and are not synced through iCloud Keychain. For keys chosen as files, only the file's location is remembered.
  • Profile activity history and build history stay local, and you can clear them in the app.
  • Your license key and check results are stored on this Mac. What the license server stores is described in Section 5.
  • The app does not delete files you placed yourself, such as .p8 files and build output.

5. License checks — what is sent

The app sends the following values to the license server only to register and check your license and update period. The website’s “get your key again”, “change your key”, “erase my data” and payment-complete pages also send only the values listed below. They are not used for any other purpose, and everything is sent over HTTPS.

Value sentWhat it isWhy it is needed
License keyThe key that identifies the license you boughtTo check that the license is valid and when the update period ends
Purchase emailThe email address you paid with. Sent only when you register in the Enter key window and when you ask for your key againTo check that the key you entered belongs to that purchase, and to send the key again only to the purchase address

What is sent, and when.

  • Activation — license key, purchase email (when registering in the Enter key window), device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version
  • License check — about every 12 hours: license key (if any), device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version
  • Registering the Mac you paid on (Register This Mac in the dialog after paying from the app) — device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version
  • Registered Macs list and deactivating another Mac — license key, device identifier, installation identifier, the list id of the Mac to deactivate
  • Deactivation — license key (if the app has it), device identifier, app version
  • Starting a payment (purchase or renewal) — license key (for renewals), device identifier, installation identifier, app version, language (for purchases). The payment itself takes place on Paddle’s checkout screen
  • Billing management link (Settings › License › “Manage billing…”) — license key (if present), device identifier, installation identifier, app version. The link we return is not stored in the app
  • Getting your key again (app and website) — purchase email, language
  • Changing your key (app and website) — purchase email and language when you ask for the confirmation email; the confirmation value and language when you change the key with the link in that email
  • Erasing your data (website) — purchase email and language when you ask for the confirmation email; the confirmation value and language when you erase with the link in that email
  • Buying on the website and the payment-complete page — the language when you start a purchase; after payment, the transaction id and a payment confirmation value, used only to show the license key you just bought on that page

What is stored on the server. Device and installation identifiers are not stored in their original form — only as hashes (SHA-256 · HMAC-SHA256). License keys are stored as a lookup hash together with an encrypted copy (AES-256-GCM) so the same key can be sent to you again; the decryption key is kept in the server’s secret settings, separate from the database, so the database alone does not reveal any license key. Payment confirmation values and email addresses used to ask for a key again or to change a key are stored only as hashes. Key-change confirmation values and replaced keys are also stored only as hashes; replaced keys are kept so we can tell a request that uses one that the key has changed. The Mac model, app version and purchase email are stored as they are; the purchase email is used to look up licenses and send keys. From Paddle we receive the customer, transaction and auto-renewal ids, the auto-renewal status (on or off), the price id and the date updates end. We do not receive names, addresses or card details.

What is not stored. IP addresses, request headers and request bodies, names and addresses, card numbers, marketing consent, usage statistics. The app does not send the macOS version.

  • Values that could identify you (user name, a name you gave the device and so on) are not used for license checks.
  • The contents of files (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) and artifact inspection results are not sent to the license server.
  • No advertising, tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs are included in the app.

6. The license server and retention

  • The license server is run by the seller, Beakbig (business registration no. 613-40-01820) · contact support@shouarchive.beakbig.com. The server uses Workers from Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), and the database is in the Asia-Pacific region (the exact location within that region is determined by Cloudflare).
  • The values sent and the license status (issued, update period, revoked) are kept for as long as the license exists. The license remains after the update period ends, so it is not deleted then. If a license is revoked because of a refund or chargeback, its records are deleted or anonymized 2 years later (for now the operator does this manually), or when you ask for deletion. Short-lived records are deleted automatically every day — access logs after 30 days, payment notification (webhook) processing records after 90 days, key delivery records after 365 days (only a hash of the recipient address is kept), unprocessed pending issuances after 7 days, records linking a payment to a Mac (payment confirmation records) after 30 days, records of deactivating other Macs after 30 days, requests to get or change a key after 1 day (only a hash of the email address is kept), and key-change confirmation links after 30 days (only a hash of the confirmation value is kept). You can erase your records yourself on the Erase my data page after confirming your purchase email (Section 11) — once erased, the app can no longer be used with that license, and only the payment records (transaction id, type, time) are kept for 5 years as required by the E-Commerce Act.
  • The values stored on this server are the same as those listed in Section 5. Payment method details, card numbers and bank accounts are not stored.
  • The server runs on Workers from Cloudflare, Inc. (a US company) in the Cloudflare data center closest to you, and the database is in the Asia-Pacific region (the exact location within that region is determined by Cloudflare). Cloudflare processes the license server and this site for transport and security purposes; the seller has Cloudflare's request logging turned off and does not store IP addresses. This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit statistics — it uses no cookies, local storage or device identifiers, and keeps only aggregate figures such as page address, referrer, browser and country, so the seller cannot see records of individual visitors. Payment is handled by Paddle as merchant of record (Section 7).
  • Every email the seller sends goes only to your purchase email address, through the email delivery service Resend, Inc. (USA). Resend receives only the recipient email address and the body of the email, which may contain the license key, a confirmation link, or the model and registration time of a newly registered Mac. The emails we send: license key · key resend · key-change confirmation · new key · new-Mac registration alert · renewal payment notice · 7-days-before-renewal notice · advance notice of a renewal price change · data erasure confirmation · purchase-email change alert (to the previous address). We send no marketing email.
  • To download the records stored on the license server or ask for them to be deleted, contact us as described in Section 11.

7. Payment and Paddle

Payment is handled by Paddle (paddle.com — Paddle.com Market Limited, London, UK; for US customers, Paddle.com Inc., USA) as the Merchant of Record. In this document, “Paddle” refers to both companies. Paddle becomes the seller to the customer and takes care of verifying payment methods, calculating and charging tax, issuing receipts and processing refunds. Payment method details are kept by Paddle and never pass through this site's servers.

  • What Paddle collects and processes — name, address, email address, payment method details and payment history, billing address, tax number, IP address, location, device and browser information, consent records and so on. These are processed under the Paddle Privacy Policy, and Paddle keeps transaction data for 5 years to meet legal obligations.
  • What is passed to the seller (me) — the buyer's name, address and email address, purchase history, and the transaction analytics visible in the dashboard. Full card numbers and security codes are not passed on. No special categories of sensitive data are shared.
  • The seller and Paddle are each independent data controllers for this data. The seller uses the information received only to issue licenses, confirm payments and respond to customer inquiries, and for no other purpose.
  • Paddle's script on the checkout page — this site's checkout page loads Paddle's payment script (Paddle.js) to open the payment window. While it runs, Paddle processes browser and device information and your IP address under its own privacy policy. Apart from a cookieless visit statistics script (Cloudflare Web Analytics, Section 6), the seller places no other scripts, and no cookies, advertising or tracking tools, on this site, and Paddle's script is not loaded unless you open the checkout page.
  • For a refund, send your payment email and order number to support@shouarchive.beakbig.com and the seller requests it from Paddle. Questions about the payment itself, such as receipts and tax, are also handled by Paddle's customer support. Refund terms are described in the Refund Policy.
  • The seller terms under which Paddle acts also apply: Paddle Terms of Service, Paddle Buyer Terms.

9. Sharing and sale

The only parties the seller shares personal information with are the license server (Cloudflare) and key delivery service (Resend) in Section 6 and Paddle in Section 7; it is not sold or handed to any other third party. Communication with Apple and Google described in Section 3 happens at your request, using your own accounts and keys. The app installer (DMG) is downloaded from Cloudflare R2, and the seller does not collect download records.

10. Children

ShouArchive is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal information, to restrict its processing, and to object to its collection (stopping processing based on decisions).

  • Erasure — records on the license server — you can do this yourself on the Erase my data page. Enter your purchase email to receive a confirmation email (valid for 24 hours); clicking “Erase” in the link from that email revokes every license under that email, deactivates all registered Macs, deletes the purchase email, stored keys, delivery records and confirmation links, and cancels automatic update renewal. Erased licenses cannot be restored. Payment records (transaction id, type, time) are kept for 5 years as required by the E-Commerce Act and then deleted.
  • Access, correction and restriction of processing, and erasure when you cannot use the erase page (for example because you no longer have access to your payment email) — send your request to support@shouarchive.beakbig.com. After verifying your identity, we handle it within 10 days of receiving the request and let you know the result.
  • Payment information kept by Paddle — handled under Paddle's policies and procedures. Please make requests directly to privacy@paddle.com or at paddle.net.
  • What you can refuse — license checks are a required process for using a license you bought. If you refuse them, you cannot use the features a license unlocks.
  • Requests made by a legal guardian or agent are handled after the authorization is confirmed.

This policy is written based on the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. Users in the EU, EEA and UK are equally guaranteed the rights provided by the GDPR and UK GDPR.

  • Requests to Apple and Google go directly to each company's servers (Section 3).
