Privacy Policy

ShouArchive (macOS) · Effective October 17, 2026 · Developer and seller: Beakbig

Summary. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts. The files you inspect are read only on your Mac. App Store Connect and Google Play features connect directly to Apple and Google with keys you register yourself. The only things that reach the seller are the license key, device identifier, Mac model and app version sent when your license is checked, the purchase email when you register, ask for your key again or change it (Section 5), and the purchase details passed on by Paddle, which handles payment as Merchant of Record (Section 7).

1. What we don't collect

The app does none of the following.

  • It does not collect analytics data such as usage statistics or crash reports.
  • It does not track you or your device, and it shows no ads.
  • There is no account or sign-in. Your license is checked with a single key.
  • It does not send the contents of the files you inspect, your project source or your build output to the seller.

2. Handling of files

The files handled by artifact inspection and profile management (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) are read only on your Mac. Their contents and anything derived from them (bundle ID, signing certificates, permission lists and so on) are only shown in the app window and are never sent anywhere. Artifact inspection does not modify the original file. Files created by building or exporting are saved only to the folder you choose.

4. What is stored on your Mac

  • App settings (theme, language, text size, project list and so on) are stored in macOS user defaults and the Application Support folder.
  • The contents of API keys are stored in this Mac's keychain and are not synced through iCloud Keychain. For keys chosen as files, only the file's location is remembered.
  • Profile activity history and build history stay local, and you can clear them in the app.
  • Your license key and check results are stored on this Mac. What the license server stores is described in Section 5.
  • The app does not delete files you placed yourself, such as .p8 files and build output.

5. License checks — what is sent

The app sends the following values to the license server only to register and check your license and update period. The website’s “get your key again” and payment-complete pages also send only the values listed below. They are not used for any other purpose, and everything is sent over HTTPS.

Value sentWhat it isWhy it is needed
License keyThe key that identifies the license you boughtTo check that the license is valid and when the update period ends
Purchase emailThe email address you paid with. Sent only when you register in the Enter key window and when you ask for your key againTo check that the key you entered belongs to that purchase, and to send the key again only to the purchase address
Device identifierThe first 32 characters of a SHA-256 hash of this Mac's hardware UUID with an app-specific prefix. The raw hardware UUID, host name and user name are not sent. If the hardware UUID can't be read, a random installation id created at first launch is used in the same way. The value stays the same even if your Mac's name or network changesTo check whether the number of devices the license allows (2 Macs) has been exceeded
Mac modelThis Mac’s hardware model name (e.g. MacBook Pro (14-inch, Nov 2023)). Not your Mac’s name or user nameTo tell the registered Macs apart in the list and let you choose which one to deactivate when all slots are taken
App versionApp version and build numberTo check whether that version was released within your update period, and to troubleshoot problems
Access logsA hash of the device identifier, app version, time and a short error reasonTo respond to outages and troubleshoot problems

What is sent, and when.

  • Activation — license key, purchase email (when registering in the Enter key window), device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version, macOS version
  • License check — about every 12 hours: license key (if any), device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version
  • Registering the Mac you paid on (Register This Mac in the dialog after paying from the app) — device identifier, installation identifier, Mac model, app version
  • Registered Macs list and deactivating another Mac — license key, device identifier, installation identifier, the list id of the Mac to deactivate
  • Deactivation — license key (if the app has it), device identifier, app version
  • Starting a payment (purchase or renewal) — license key (for renewals), device identifier, installation identifier, app version, language (for purchases). The payment itself takes place on Paddle’s checkout screen
  • Getting your key again (app and website) — purchase email, language
  • Changing your key (app and website) — purchase email and language when you ask for the confirmation email; the confirmation value and language when you change the key with the link in that email
  • Buying on the website and the payment-complete page — the language when you start a purchase; after payment, the transaction id and a payment confirmation value, used only to show the license key you just bought on that page

What is stored on the server. Device and installation identifiers are not stored in their original form — only as hashes (SHA-256 · HMAC-SHA256). License keys are stored as a lookup hash together with an encrypted copy (AES-256-GCM) so the same key can be sent to you again; the decryption key is kept in the server’s secret settings, separate from the database, so the database alone does not reveal any license key. Payment confirmation values and email addresses used to ask for a key again or to change a key are stored only as hashes. Key-change confirmation values and replaced keys are also stored only as hashes; replaced keys are kept so we can tell a request that uses one that the key has changed. The Mac model, app version and purchase email are stored as they are; the purchase email is used to look up licenses and send keys. From Paddle we receive the customer, transaction and auto-renewal ids, the auto-renewal status (on or off), the price id and the date updates end. We do not receive names, addresses or card details.

What is not stored. IP addresses, request headers and request bodies, macOS version (received but not stored), names and addresses, card numbers, marketing consent, usage statistics.

  • Values that could identify you (user name, a name you gave the device and so on) are not used for license checks.
  • The contents of files (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) and artifact inspection results are not sent to the license server.
  • No advertising, tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs are included in the app.

6. The license server and retention

  • The license server is run by the seller, Beakbig (business registration no. 613-40-01820) · contact support@beakbig.com. The server uses Workers from Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), and the database is in the Asia-Pacific region (the exact location within that region is determined by Cloudflare).
  • The values sent and the license status (issued, update period, revoked) are kept for as long as the license exists. The license remains after the update period ends, so it is not deleted then. If a license is revoked because of a refund or chargeback, its records are deleted or anonymized 2 years later (for now the operator does this manually), or when you ask for deletion. Short-lived records are deleted automatically every day — access logs after 30 days, payment notification (webhook) processing records after 90 days, key delivery records after 365 days (only a hash of the recipient address is kept), unprocessed pending issuances after 7 days, records linking a payment to a Mac (payment confirmation records) after 30 days, records of deactivating other Macs after 30 days, requests to get or change a key after 1 day (only a hash of the email address is kept), and key-change confirmation links after 30 days (only a hash of the confirmation value is kept). When we receive a deletion request, we verify your identity, revoke the license and delete the remaining records — once deleted, the app can no longer be used with that license.
  • The values stored on this server are the same as those listed in Section 5. Payment method details, card numbers and bank accounts are not stored.
  • The server runs on Workers from Cloudflare, Inc. (a US company) in the Cloudflare data center closest to you, and the database is in the Asia-Pacific region (the exact location within that region is determined by Cloudflare). Payment is handled by Paddle.com Market Limited (UK) as merchant of record.
  • License key emails, key-change confirmation emails and new-Mac registration alerts are sent through the email delivery service Resend, Inc. (USA). Resend receives only the recipient email address and the body of the email, which may contain the license key, a key-change confirmation link, or the model and registration time of a newly registered Mac.
  • To download the records stored on the license server or ask for them to be deleted, contact us as described in Section 11.

7. Payment and Paddle

Payment is handled by Paddle (paddle.com) as the Merchant of Record. Paddle becomes the seller to the customer and takes care of verifying payment methods, calculating and charging tax, issuing receipts and processing refunds. Payment method details are kept by Paddle and never pass through this site's servers.

  • What Paddle collects and processes — name, address, email address, payment method details and payment history, billing address, tax number, IP address, location, device and browser information, consent records and so on. These are processed under the Paddle Privacy Policy, and Paddle keeps transaction data for 5 years to meet legal obligations.
  • What is passed to the seller (me) — the buyer's name, address and email address, purchase history, and the transaction analytics visible in the dashboard. Full card numbers and security codes are not passed on. No special categories of sensitive data are shared.
  • The seller and Paddle are each independent data controllers for this data. The seller uses the information received only to issue licenses, confirm payments and respond to customer inquiries, and for no other purpose.
  • Questions about payments are handled by Paddle's customer support. Refund terms are described in the Refund Policy.
  • The seller terms under which Paddle acts also apply: Paddle Terms of Service, Paddle Buyer Terms.

8. Third-party libraries

  • Lottie — an open-source library for showing loading animations. It is used only for display and neither connects to the network nor collects data.
  • Sparkle — an open-source library that checks for and installs app updates. It is used only for the update check in Section 3, and sending system information is not turned on.

There are no other third-party SDKs that communicate over the network.

9. Sharing and sale

The only parties the seller shares personal information with are the license server (Cloudflare) and key delivery service (Resend) in Section 6 and Paddle in Section 7; it is not sold or handed to any other third party. Communication with Apple and Google described in Section 3 happens at your request, using your own accounts and keys.

10. Children

ShouArchive is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 14.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal information, to restrict its processing, and to object to its collection (stopping processing based on decisions).

  • Records on the license server — send your request to support@beakbig.com. After verifying your identity, we handle it within 10 days of receiving the request and let you know the result.
  • Payment information kept by Paddle — handled under Paddle's policies and procedures. Please make requests for that information directly to Paddle.
  • What you can refuse — license checks are a required process for using a license you bought. If you refuse them, you cannot use the features a license unlocks.
  • Requests made by a legal guardian or agent are handled after the authorization is confirmed.

This policy is written based on the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. Users in the EU, EEA and UK are equally guaranteed the rights provided by the GDPR and UK GDPR.

12. International transfers

  • Recipients and countries — license records are processed by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), payment information by Paddle.com Market Limited (UK), and key delivery emails by Resend, Inc. (USA).
  • Storage location — license records are stored in a database in the Asia-Pacific region (Cloudflare D1), and requests are processed in the Cloudflare data center closest to you.
  • When and how — sent over the network when you activate, when your license is checked, when you view the registered Macs, when you ask for your key again or change it, when you start a payment (purchase or renewal), and when a license key, a key-change confirmation or a new-Mac registration alert is emailed to you.
  • Items, purposes and retention — as described in Sections 5 and 6.
  • Safeguards — Cloudflare follows its data processing terms (including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses), and Paddle, as Merchant of Record, processes payment information under its own privacy policy.
  • How to refuse, and the consequences — if you do not use a license, no transfer takes place. In that case, after the trial period only profile management and artifact inspection can be used.
  • Requests to Apple and Google go directly to each company's servers (Section 3).
