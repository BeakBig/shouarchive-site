Privacy Policy

ShouArchive for macOS · Effective October 3, 2026 · Developer: BeakBig · Mac App Store edition and the commercial edition

This document covers both editions. The Mac App Store edition (sections 1 – 7) collects no personal data, just as it does today: no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts, the files you inspect are read on your Mac only, and the network is used only when you register your own App Store Connect API key and the app talks to Apple on your behalf. The commercial edition (sections 9 – 13) is the edition you subscribe to directly on the web; it sends a license key, device identifier and app version to the license server only when it checks your license, and payment is handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Section 8 on changes and section 14 on contact apply to both editions. The two editions are not linked to each other.

1. Data we collect — Mac App Store edition

In this edition, there is none. The app does not:

  • collect analytics such as usage statistics or crash reports;
  • track you or your device, or show ads;
  • require an account or sign-in;
  • include any third-party SDK that communicates over the network.

2. Files you open

Files handled by artifact inspection and profile management (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) are read on your Mac only. Their contents and anything derived from them (bundle IDs, signing certificates, permission lists and so on) are shown in the app window and are never transmitted anywhere. Inspection never modifies the original file.

3. Network use

The app uses the network in exactly one case: when you register your own App Store Connect API key (Issuer ID, Key ID and .p8 file) and use a feature that needs it, the app connects to Apple's App Store Connect API at api.appstoreconnect.apple.com.

FeatureWhat is sent to Apple
List and download profilesAn authentication token signed by the app
Create, regenerate or renew profilesThe token plus the profile type, bundle ID, certificates and devices you selected
List and register devicesThe token plus the device names and UDIDs you entered
  • The .p8 private key stays on your Mac. The app stores only a reference to the file (a macOS security-scoped bookmark) and signs request tokens locally. The key itself is never sent anywhere, including to Apple.
  • Apple's handling of these requests is governed by the Apple Developer Program agreements and Apple's privacy policy.
  • If you never register an API key, the app makes no network connections at all.

4. Data stored on your Mac

  • App settings (theme, language, text size, API key details and similar) are stored in the app's sandbox container provided by macOS.
  • Access to the profile folder and to .p8 files is remembered with security-scoped bookmarks that you grant in the open panel. You can remove them in the app at any time.
  • The profile activity history (downloads, creations, device registrations and so on) is kept locally and can be cleared with "Clear History".
  • Deleting the app removes its container and this data with it. Files you placed yourself, such as the .p8 file, are never deleted by the app.

5. Third-party libraries

The app bundles the open-source library Lottie to play a loading animation. It is used for display only and does not access the network or collect data. There are no other third-party SDKs.

6. Sharing and selling

The Mac App Store edition collects nothing, so nothing is shared with or sold to anyone. The communication with Apple described in section 3 happens at your request, using your own account and key. In the commercial edition, the only recipients are the license server in section 11 and Paddle in section 12; nothing is sold or passed on to any other third party[Confirm against the implementation].

7. Children

ShouArchive is a tool for software developers and is not directed at children. In the Mac App Store edition it collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If the way the app handles data actually changes, the app's release notes will say so as well.

9. Commercial edition — data we collect

The commercial edition (the one you subscribe to directly on this site) sends the values below to the license server only to verify your license while your subscription is active. They are not used for anything else, and every transmission is over HTTPS.

Value sentWhat it isWhy it is needed
License keyThe key that identifies your subscriptionTo check whether the subscription is valid
Device identifierThe first 32 characters of the SHA-256 hash of this Mac's hardware UUID with an app-specific prefix added. The raw hardware UUID, the hostname and the user name are not sent. If the hardware UUID cannot be read, the app hashes a random install ID created on the first launch in the same way. The value stays the same even if the Mac name or the network changesTo check whether you have gone over the number of devices your plan allows
App versionApp version · build numberTo confirm the right features and to solve problems
Access logA hash of the device identifier, the app version, the time and a short error reasonTo respond to outages and to solve problems

What is sent and when.

  • Activation — license key, purchase email, device identifier, installation identifier, app version, macOS version
  • License verification — roughly every 12 hours: license key, device identifier, installation identifier, app version
  • Deactivation — license key, device identifier
  • Starting a payment — plan (monthly · annual), device identifier, installation identifier, app version. The payment itself takes place on Paddle's checkout page

What is stored on the server. The license key and the device and installation identifiers are not stored as they are — only a hash (SHA-256 · HMAC-SHA256) is stored. The app version and the purchase email are stored as they are, and the purchase email is used to look up licenses and to send keys. From Paddle we receive the subscription, customer and transaction IDs, the subscription status, the price ID, the next billing date and any scheduled change. We do not receive names, addresses or card information.

What is not stored. IP addresses, request headers and request bodies, the macOS version (received but not stored), names and addresses, card numbers, marketing consent and usage statistics.

  • These values are sent only by the commercial edition. The Mac App Store edition makes no network connections (section 3), so it sends nothing.
  • Values that can identify you (your name, a name you gave a device yourself, and so on) are not used for license verification.
  • The contents of files (IPA, APK, AAB, .mobileprovision) and artifact inspection results are not sent to the license server.
  • Advertising, tracking and behavioral analytics SDKs are not included in the commercial edition either[Confirm against the implementation].

10. Commercial edition — license server and retention

  • The license server is operated by BeakBig (an individual, not yet registered as a business) · contact beakbigkb@gmail.com. The server uses the Workers and D1 services of Cloudflare, Inc. (United States)[database location — decided when the server is built].
  • The values sent and the license status (issued, expired, canceled) are kept for as long as the subscription is active, and are deleted or anonymized two years after the subscription ends or is canceled (this is currently done by the operator by hand). Shorter-lived records are deleted automatically every day — access logs after 30 days, processed payment notification (webhook) records after 90 days, key delivery records after 365 days (only a hash of the address received is kept), and unprocessed pending issuances after 7 days. If we receive a cancellation request, we suspend the license as soon as it is confirmed and delete the remaining records.
  • The values stored on this server are the ones listed in section 9. Payment method details, card numbers and bank accounts are not stored.
  • The server and the database are located at Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), and payments are handled by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom) as merchant of record.
  • The email delivery service used to send keys: [email sending service — not chosen yet]
  • To download the history held on the license server or ask for its deletion, contact us using the method in section 12.

11. Commercial edition — payment and Paddle

Payment for the commercial edition is handled by Paddle (paddle.com) as the Merchant of Record. Paddle becomes the seller to the customer and takes care of payment method verification, calculating and applying tax, issuing receipts and processing refunds. Paddle keeps the payment method details, and they never pass through this site's servers.

  • What Paddle collects and processes — name, address, email address, payment method details and payment history, billing address, tax number, IP address, location, device and browser information, consent records and so on. This is handled under the Paddle Privacy Policy, and Paddle keeps transaction data for 5 years to meet its legal obligations.
  • What comes to the seller (us) — the buyer's name, address and email address, the purchase history, and the transaction analytics visible in the dashboard. Full card numbers and security codes do not come through. No special categories of sensitive information are shared.
  • The seller and Paddle are each an independent data controller for this data. The seller uses what it receives only to issue licenses, confirm payment and answer customer questions.
  • Questions about payment are handled by Paddle's customer support. The refund criteria are written up in the Refund Policy.
  • Paddle's reseller terms apply as well: Paddle Terms of Service, Paddle Buyer Terms.

12. Your rights as a data subject

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and refuse the processing of your personal data (suspension of processing in certain cases).

  • Records held on the license server — send your request to beakbigkb@gmail.com. Once we have confirmed your identity, we will process it within 10 days of receiving the request and let you know the outcome.
  • Payment information held by Paddle — handled under Paddle's own policy and procedures. If you need that information, request it from Paddle directly.
  • What you can refuse — license verification is the processing required to use the commercial edition. If you refuse it, you cannot use the commercial edition.
  • If a parent or legal representative makes the request on your behalf, we confirm the relationship first and then handle it.

This policy is written on the basis of the Republic of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. For users in the EU, EEA and the UK, the rights set by the GDPR and the UK GDPR are guaranteed as well.

13. Transfers outside your country

  • Recipients and countries — license records for the commercial edition are handled by Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), and payment information by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom).
  • When and how — sent over the network when you activate, verify your license and start a payment.
  • Items, purposes and retention periods — the same as those set out in sections 9 and 10.
  • Safeguards — Cloudflare follows its data processing agreement (including the EU standard contractual clauses), and Paddle handles payment information under its own privacy policy as merchant of record.
  • How to refuse, and what happens then — if you do not use a commercial edition license, no transfer takes place. In that case the build feature of the direct distribution edition is locked once the trial period ends, and the App Store edition does not use this server.
  • App Store Connect API requests sent to Apple go to Apple's servers (section 3).

14. Contact

Questions about this policy or about how the app handles data: beakbigkb@gmail.com.
