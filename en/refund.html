2. Who handles the payment

Payment for the commercial edition is handled by Paddle as the Merchant of Record. Paddle verifies payment methods, calculates and applies taxes, issues receipts and makes the final decision on refunds. That is also why refund requests have to go through Paddle, and why any refund amount is returned to you by Paddle rather than sent by the seller directly.

Two important points about the relationship with Paddle. Paddle operates a standard refund policy (paddle.com/legal/refund-policy). Unless the law requires otherwise, that policy says that if a request arrives within 14 days of the transaction, Paddle may refund at its own discretion, but a request does not by itself guarantee a refund. Statutory minimum periods in each region take priority over that: 7 days in Korea, Brazil and China; 14 days in the EU, the UK, Switzerland, Turkey and Israel; 7 days in Canada; 5 days in Singapore. This document is no less favorable than Paddle's policy.

3. Trial period

  • If you cancel before your 14-day trial ends, you are not charged. There is no charge during the trial.
  • Once the trial has ended and the subscription started automatically, there are cases where you can still withdraw within 14 days. In some regions such as the EU and the UK, the 14-day withdrawal period starts again from the moment the trial ends[Finalize this based on Paddle's policy and the law in each region].
  • If you have already started using the subscription, the right of withdrawal may not apply (Paddle's policy excepts cases where use of digital content has begun).
  • After 14 days, section 4 below applies.

4. Refunds for a paid subscription

  • Monthly subscription — no partial refunds. You can cancel at any time, and cancelling means you keep using it until the end of the period you already paid for. It does not renew after that, and the unused remainder is not refunded.
  • Where the law says otherwise, that applies instead. Consumers in the EU, EEA, Switzerland and the UK can withdraw within 14 days of their first payment, and anywhere else the withdrawal rights set by law take precedence. If you withdraw within the minimum period the law allows, you get back the amount you paid minus the charges for the period you already used.
  • If you ask within 14 days of the payment and have not used the app yet, you can request a full refund. Paddle makes the final decision.
  • Annual subscription — we refund the remaining time. The refund is what you paid minus the number of months you have already used multiplied by the monthly price. The month you started counts as a full month. If the result is zero or less, there is no refund.
  • When a refund is processed, that license is suspended and cannot be used again.

5. When you get a full refund

  • The same payment was processed twice.
  • A different product was charged than the one being sold.
  • The amount charged differs from the price listed on the Pricing page.
  • Your payment details were recorded incorrectly, so the payment did not go through.
  • A service outage that left the app unusable continued through the period — in that case we extend the period or refund it in full.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Go through Paddle. Use the support link on your payment receipt or the billing page in your account, or choose "Request refund" on paddle.net[Put the actual support link from the receipt and account pages you get after paying here].
  2. Including your email address and the payment method you used makes it easier to find your payment. Since Paddle processes the payment, we cannot process a refund directly at beakbigkb@gmail.com. You are welcome to write to that address when you want us to look into the cause, though.
  3. Paddle reviews the request and decides on one of three things: a full refund, a partial refund or a rejection.
  4. Card refunds usually take 3–5 business days. Only card transactions within 120 days of the payment date can be refunded (179 days for PayPal), and the refund amount goes back to the original payment method. When the money actually arrives depends on the card issuer and the payment processor.

Tax (VAT, GST) deducted by a refund is handled the way the tax authority requires[Check how tax refunds are processed].

7. When there is no refund

  • Charges for a period you used normally.
  • Duplicate use confirmed through a shared license key or account.
  • Breaching the Terms of Service, for example by selling or redistributing the app.
  • The app cannot run in your computer environment (please check the requirements).
  • Cases where a refund is not possible under Paddle's or the payment provider's rules.

In regions where the law grants rights that take priority over this, that law applies.

8. Cancelling and getting a refund are different

Cancelling a subscription stops the payment from the next renewal on; it is not a refund for the period you already paid for. To get that period back, request a refund under sections 4 and 5 above. You can cancel from the subscription notice Paddle sends you (by email or in the billing page for your payment method).

9. Contact

If you have a question about refunds, write to beakbigkb@gmail.com. For anything else, see the Support page.
