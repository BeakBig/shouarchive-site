What happens when you change it

  • The old key stops working. Macs that try to register with it are told that the key has changed.
  • All registered Macs are released. Register the Macs you keep using again with the link in the new key email.
  • The new key is sent only to the email address you used to pay. This page never shows the key.
  • Your update period and the number of Macs you can use (2) stay the same.

Good to know

  • You can change the key even with no Macs registered — for example, if you replaced or lost your Mac.
  • If you only lost your key, there’s no need to change it: get the same key from Get your license key again.
  • From the second Mac on, we email you whenever a new Mac is registered. If it wasn’t you, you can change the key right away with the link in that email.
  • If you can no longer use the email you paid with, send the payment date and what appears on your card statement to support@shouarchive.beakbig.com. Once we confirm it, we’ll change your purchase email.
